HolaRise — Together We Rise, Together We Serve

Born on the quiet morning hills of Hoa Lac, HolaRise is an independent, non-profit youth initiative uniting students and young professionals to cultivate inner stillness (HolaHeart), care for natural landscapes (HolaGreen), and share knowledge with local communities (HolaConnect).

Who We Are

Rooted in Hoa Lac · Built for a Broader Horizon

Hoa Lac — home to Vietnam National University (VNU) campuses, FPT University, and the Hoa Lac Hi-Tech Park — is our launchpad and first proving ground. Yet our mission is not bounded by a single district: we walk alongside young people so that values of clarity, ecological care, and civic service travel with them wherever they live and work.

01 · HOLAHEART

Inner Stillness & Peer Listening

Intimate, screen-free listening circles and acoustic evenings where young people reflect on family gratitude, personal resilience, and honest conversation — held strictly as peer-support spaces, never clinical therapy.

02 · HOLAGREEN

Ecology & Sustainable Landscapes

Practical environmental stewardship focused on safe public shoreline and roadside cleanups, single-use plastic reduction, waste sorting at source, and native greenery care.

03 · HOLACONNECT

Shared Knowledge & Community Service

Bringing youth energy beyond university gates through outdoor pine-hill treks, children's community bookshelves, weekend reading hours, and transparent collaborations with local schools and cultural spaces.

Governance & Safeguarding

Transparency & Ethical Standards

In-Kind First & Open Traceability

HolaRise operates on a strict non-profit, non-commercial basis. We prioritize in-kind partnerships (venues, books, saplings, learning supplies) over ad-hoc cash collection, and publish clear delivery summaries after each campaign.

Ethical Storytelling & Safeguarding

We uphold dignity-first photography and strict child safeguarding: no exploitative imagery, no identifiable photos of minors without explicit guardian/school consent, and minimal personal data collection compliant with Vietnam Decree 13/2023/ND-CP.

Note on event language: Regular weekly/monthly gatherings in Hoa Lac are currently conducted in Vietnamese unless explicitly noted as bilingual on the invitation.
