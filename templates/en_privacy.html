Transparency, Safeguarding & Privacy Standards

{{BRAND_EN_NAME}} operates strictly as a non-profit initiative, placing human dignity, open resource traceability, and personal data protection at the heart of every gathering.

1. Non-Profit Operations & Resource Transparency

  • Free admission & clear upfront costs: Every HolaRise gathering is free to attend. Any self-paid cafe drink or shared supply cost is stated clearly on the invitation before you reserve a seat.
  • In-kind first & open traceability: We prioritize in-kind contributions (venues, books, saplings, learning materials) over informal personal cash collection. Every campaign publishes a clear handover summary.
  • Strictly non-commercial: HolaRise operates independently with zero hidden commercial pitches, upsells, or unauthorized fundraising.
  • Verified documentation only: We only publish real field counts and photographs from actual gatherings; upcoming tracks are always labeled clearly as preparing to launch.

2. Ethical Storytelling & Psychological / Child Safeguarding

  • Peer-listening boundary (HolaHeart): Our dialogue circles are voluntary peer-support spaces — never clinical counseling or psychotherapy. Every guest freely decides whether to speak or simply listen.
  • Dignity & child safeguarding (HolaConnect): We never publish close-up photos of distressed participants for engagement, and never publish identifiable images or personal details of minors without explicit guardian or school consent.
  • Personal image control: Full-resolution event albums are shared privately within each session's group rather than opened for public scraping. Any participant may request editing, blurring, or removal of a photo featuring them at any time.

3. Personal Data Protection & Anonymous Notes

In alignment with Vietnam Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection, HolaRise collects only the minimal details required to coordinate seating and send gathering updates (your name and phone, Zalo, or email). We never sell, rent, or transfer personal data to third parties.

Reflections or pre-event notes marked anonymous are strictly separated from your name and contact details before reading or publishing. Temporary local form drafts on your browser are automatically cleared upon submission.

4. Gathering Language Policy

Regular weekly circles in Hoa Lac are primarily conducted in Vietnamese unless marked bilingual on the invitation. International students, expats, and English-speaking guests are always warmly welcomed, and coordinators can assist with orientation and light translation upon note in your RSVP.
